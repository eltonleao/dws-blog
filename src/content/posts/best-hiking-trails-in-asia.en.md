# Best Hiking Trails in Asia

Asia holds some of the most varied hiking on the planet, from high mountain passes to forest pilgrim paths and tropical summits. The best trails reward careful planning, and knowing what makes each one special helps you pick the route that fits your fitness, your season and your taste for adventure.

In Nepal, the Annapurna Circuit loops around an entire massif and crosses a very high pass along the way. Its appeal lies in the variety: rice terraces and subtropical forest at the start, dry high valleys and glaciers later on. Simple lodges along the trail serve hot meals, so you can travel light. The main challenge is altitude. Climb slowly, add rest days, and turn back if headache, nausea or confusion get worse, because the only reliable cure for altitude sickness is going down.

Japan offers a gentler kind of hike on the Kumano Kodo, a network of ancient pilgrimage routes through the forested mountains of the Kii Peninsula. Stone paths climb past cedar trees, small shrines and villages where family-run inns serve dinner and a hot bath. The stages are shorter but often steep, and rain is frequent, so pack shoes with good grip and a light rain jacket.

In southwest China, the high trail of Tiger Leaping Gorge runs along a cliff face far above a roaring river, with snowy peaks on both sides and guesthouses to break up the walk. In Malaysian Borneo, Mount Kinabalu is a steep climb through rainforest and cloud forest to a granite summit, usually reached before dawn. Climbers must go with a licensed guide and book a permit and a mountain hut well ahead, as places are limited.

Whatever trail you choose, timing matters most. Monsoon rains close or spoil many Himalayan routes in summer, while autumn and spring bring clear skies. Check permit rules before you go, carry cash for remote villages and respect local customs at temples and shrines. Pack out your rubbish, stay on the marked path and consider hiring local guides and porters: they make the trip safer and keep the money in the mountain communities that host you.
