# Summer Fashion Essentials

A good summer wardrobe rests on a handful of essentials that help you handle the heat, protect you from the sun and mix with each other without effort. Get the fabrics and the fit right, and you will spend less time choosing outfits on hot mornings and more time enjoying the season.

In the heat, fabric matters more than anything else. Your body cools itself as sweat evaporates, so clothes need to let air move and moisture escape. Linen and loosely woven cotton do this well: linen dries quickly and stands away from the skin, which leaves room for a breeze. Tight synthetic knits hold sweat against the body and turn sticky before long. Light colors reflect more sunlight, but a loose cut matters even more, because it lets the heat out.

With that in mind, a few pieces cover most summer days. Two linen shirts in pale tones work open over a tank top or buttoned with tailored shorts. Lightweight chinos or wide leg trousers take you from the office to dinner. A simple cotton dress or a relaxed jumpsuit is a complete outfit in a single piece. Add plain tees in good cotton, shorts that end just above the knee and a light layer for air conditioned rooms.

Shoes and accessories finish the look and do real work. Leather sandals and canvas sneakers let your feet breathe; save heavy boots for autumn. A wide brim hat shades the face, neck and ears far better than a cap, and good sunglasses with full ultraviolet protection look after your eyes, not just your style. A straw or canvas tote carries sunscreen, water and a spare shirt.

Before buying, check the fiber content and hold the fabric up to the light: a clear, even weave breathes, while a dense one will feel warm. Pick a short palette, such as white, sand, navy and one bright accent, so every top goes with every bottom. Wash linen in cool water and hang it to dry; its soft creases are part of its charm. Keep the essentials simple, and let one bold piece carry the personality of each outfit.
